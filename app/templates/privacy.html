Privacy & cookies

Your data

GUIDANCE3 is free and open to all users, and no login or registration is required. Each job's input and results are available only through the job's unique results link. Only you and anyone you share that link with can see them. Your email address is optional and is used only to tell you when your job has finished.

Cookies

GUIDANCE3 does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Session cookie (strictly necessary). The server may set a session cookie that is deleted when you close your browser. It is not used to track you.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (optional, third-party). To block automated job submissions, the submission form can use Google reCAPTCHA, which sets cookies from Google. reCAPTCHA is loaded only if you click "Accept reCAPTCHA cookies"; until then, no request is sent to Google. {% if declined_per_hour and declined_per_hour > 0 and declined_per_day and declined_per_day > 0 %} If you decline, everything else works as usual (browsing, viewing and downloading results), and you can submit up to {{ declined_per_hour }} job{{ 's' if declined_per_hour != 1 }} per hour and {{ declined_per_day }} per day from your network address. {% else %} If you decline, everything else works as usual (browsing, viewing and downloading results), but submitting a job requires reCAPTCHA. {% endif %} See Google's privacy policy and terms of service.
  • Submission limit without reCAPTCHA. To apply this limit, the server keeps a one-way (salted) hash of your network address and the times of your submissions for 24 hours. The address itself is not stored.
  • Your cookie choice. Your accept or decline choice is stored in your browser's local storage, so the banner is not shown again; after 12 months you are asked again. It is not sent to our server.
